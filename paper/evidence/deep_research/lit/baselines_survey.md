## 3. Learned methods compared against NOMP (all found)
- R23 and R24 run NOMP. Both keep Newton/NOMP refinement inside their own pipeline: the network only replaces NOMP's coarse detection. R23 matches NOMP accuracy at much lower run time. R24 beats NNOMP only after its NOMP refiner; the raw CKNet output is worse than NNOMP.
- R25 (abstract only) adds DL to NOMP because "the performance of NOMP deteriorates severely" at low SNR. R22 is the closest pure-CNN case, and its baseline is iterative ML, not NOMP: the CNN alone saturates and reaches the CRB only after Newton refinement.
- No NOMP in DeepFreq (R14), SignalNet (R16), SwinFreq (R17), SAFE (R18) or NeuPLSE (R19); all have 0 NOMP hits, and NeuPLSE omits NOMP even from its references (cResFreq R15 was not read). R26 calls NOMP state of the art but does not run it.
- Searches turned up unfolded OMP/MP networks (e.g., MOMPnet, R32) but no unfolded or "learned NOMP". I found no paper comparing a pure network against NOMP for analog-mmWave joint AoA/AoD.

## 4. Reasons for the omission: what sources state vs my inference
- STATED, baseline lineage: R1 chose TSDCE/DFT-CEA because earlier work showed TSDCE beat LS and OMP, so the chain never includes NOMP or ANM. R4-R6 compare only with on-grid OMP/AMP, and R6 notes that grid dictionaries miss off-grid AoA/AoD.
- STATED, speed concerns: R23 says NOMP's O(LMN log MN) cost leads to "considerably long" processing. R26 says NOMP/RELAX are "time-consuming when the model order is large" and ANM (SDP) is "computationally complicated". R6 says optimization algorithms "demand considerable computational complexity". R14 times CBLasso at 1.71 s. Counter-evidence: R27 reports that NOMP is faster than AST, Lasso and MUSIC in all four of its scenarios.
- STATED, problem framing: covariance/multi-snapshot DOA papers (R7-R9) benchmark against the MUSIC family. R14 says the only count estimators "rely on an eigendecomposition", but NOMP already had a CFAR stopping rule (R27). R16 says no algorithm handled quantized data, but VALSE-EP (R28, gridless, quantized) appeared in 2018. The date conflicts are my reading.
- STATED, DL vs strong parametric methods: where a strong parametric method is included, the network loses or ties at high SNR (R13, R14, R21, R22, R23).
- STATED, general critiques: R29 says a trained network cannot beat a known optimal algorithm; DL helps mainly to cut complexity or to handle nonlinear hardware. R30: neural-IR gains often come from weak or untuned baselines. R31: DL PHY research neglects the accuracy-generalization trade-off. NULL: I found no published critique that names the missing NOMP/ANM baselines in DL AoA/AoD work.
- INFERENCE (mine): NOMP comes from the frequency-estimation/radar literature, while mmWave DL papers inherit OMP/AMP baselines from compressed-sensing channel estimation. Of the 20+ learned-method texts checked, only R23 and R24 run NOMP, and both use Newton refinement in their own pipeline. R26 mentions NOMP; the rest never cite it. Side information is not the reason: L/K is already given to the baselines (R1, R8, R14, R16), so NOMP with known L would be a like-for-like comparison. A weak-baseline publication incentive (R30) would be consistent with this pattern but is not proven.

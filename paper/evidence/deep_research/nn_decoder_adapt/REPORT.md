# Adapting the learned (unfolded) decoder to low-resolution ADCs

Folder: `D:/ai_ml_project/paper/evidence/deep_research/nn_decoder_adapt/`. Every number below comes from `ld_adapt_adc_run.json` / `.log` (main run), `ld_compare.json` / `.log` (pairing with the ADC stage) and `ld_replicate.json` / `.log` (fresh-seed replicate); this file is generated by `make_report.py`. Pd = paper-style Pd (both angles within 1 deg, mean over SNR groups); true L = 3 given to every method. Paired bootstrap: 2000 resamples within SNR groups, seed 0; `*` = 95% CI excludes 0.

## Short answer

- **Adapting the learned decoder helps, but only relative to other decoders.** At 1-2 bits the adapted decoder beats NOMP (+0.02 to +0.08) and the original learned decoder (+0.02 to +0.07) on both the stress seeds and new seeds, all with CIs excluding 0. At 3 bits / 15 dB the gain is small (+0.01 to +0.02). At 4 bits, with no ADC, and at 3 bits / 0 dB it ties or loses slightly (-0.006 to -0.007 vs NOMP, sometimes significant).
- **It does not beat the best classical method, except in one cell.** The best classical method (quantisation-consistent NOMP / parabolic, classical, no learning; finetune/adc) is significantly better in 5 of the 8 ADC cells on the stress seeds (by 0.03 to 0.18 Pd) and in 7 of 8 on the new seeds. The only cell where the adapted all-neural pipeline wins against the best classical method is b = 1, 15 dB: +0.029 [+0.015, +0.043] on the stress seeds and +0.036 [+0.025, +0.047] on new seeds. At b4/0 dB and b3/0 dB it ties the best classical method on the stress seeds and loses slightly on new seeds (-0.007*).
- **It loses to the fine-tuned CNN read out directly ("FT network only", no decoder) at every bit depth of 2 or fewer**, by 0.03 to 0.24 Pd. Running any decoder (classical or learned, original or adapted) on the network output costs Pd under coarse quantisation. So the best neural system at b <= 2 is still network-only, not CNN + adapted decoder.
- **Cost on the official subset (2000 scenes): small and significant.** Adapted pipeline 0.748 vs NOMP 0.757: -0.009 [-0.013, -0.005]*. Against the best classical method there (NOMP os8 rc6, 0.758) the gap is -0.011*. It is identical to the original learned pipeline (+0.000 [-0.002, +0.002]), so adapting causes no regression; the deficit to NOMP was already there. On the clean stress banks it is -0.008* (15 dB) and -0.004 (0 dB, n.s.) vs NOMP.

## What was run

- `ld_adapt_adc.py` fine-tunes StagedSC(RefinerV3) (4,803 params) from `learned_decoder/lurd.pt`. Setup: 1500 steps, batch 64, Adam 1e-3 with cosine decay, 6 threads, 25.4 min, run detached. Batches are 50% the original training distribution (phase <= 8 deg, gain <= 3 dB, SNR -15..24, L = 3) and 50% the same passed through `stress_sim.adc(Y, b, "peak")` with b ~ U{1..4}. Heat seeds come from the fine-tuned CNN `finetune/adc/cache/adc_ft.pt` (loaded read-only; final.pt and lurd.pt untouched). Training batch at step s uses `default_rng([61000000, s])`; this seed is used nowhere else (grep).
- Training loss (avg of last 50 steps) went from 3.85 (step 50) to 3.69 (step 1500), noisy between 3.51 and 3.89. The learned self-calibration strength went from 1.01 to 1.03. Adapted weights: `cache/ld_adc_final_run.pt`; resumable checkpoint: `cache/ld_adc_run.pt`.
- Evaluation on the same Y for all methods:
  - stress M5 banks: seeds 5015 / 5000, 500 scenes, b = 4, 3, 2, 1, plus the clean banks;
  - official subset: first 250 scenes per SNR of `eval_bank.npz`, 2000 scenes;
  - fresh replicate: seeds 8315 / 8300, 1000 scenes; these are the ADC stage's new banks, evaluated with no retraining.
- In every bank, my NOMP per-scene stats are identical to the ADC stage's, which was asserted. That makes the pairing with the ADC stage's classical baselines, FT network only and FT dual+SC exact, scene by scene.
- Best classical = the highest Pd among 13 classical methods, picked on the test bank itself, which favours the classical side. The 13 are: NOMP, NOMP+SC, NOMP+SC tuned, FFT-peak seeds (+SC), classical dual (+SC), NOMP os8 rc6, multi-start K6, consistent NOMP, FFT parabolic os4/os16 and consistent parabolic.
- Fixes made to the pre-written script (the original is kept as `ld_adapt_adc_v1.py`):
  - NOMP and the decoders now run on chunks of at most 400 scenes. v1 ran nomp_greedy on 2000 scenes at once on the official subset, which breaks the memory rule.
  - Threads raised from 4 to 6.
  - Per-scene stats are now saved so the results can be paired with the ADC stage.
  - Nothing else needed changing. The seeds are disjoint, Y is the same for all methods, the evaluator is the same scene_stats/boot_metrics, and the adc_ft.pt loading was checked. The re-run smoke test (`ld_adapt_adc_smoke.log`) reproduced the first smoke test exactly.

## Verdict

- **vs NOMP:** the adapted all-neural pipeline (FT-CNN + adapted learned decoder) beats NOMP under low-resolution ADCs at b = 1, 2 (both SNRs) and b = 3 at 15 dB, on both seed sets (CIs exclude 0). The margin is +0.07 to +0.08 at b <= 2 / 15 dB, +0.02 to +0.03 at 0 dB and +0.02 at b3 / 15 dB. It ties NOMP at b = 4 / 15 dB. It is slightly worse at b = 3-4 / 0 dB (-0.006 to -0.007; significant only on the fresh seeds).
- **vs the best classical method:** it does not beat it in general. The best classical method is significantly better in 5 of 8 ADC cells on the stress seeds (7 of 8 on the new seeds), by 0.007 to 0.18 Pd (largest at b3 / 15 dB, -0.18*, consistent NOMP). The one replicated win is b = 1 / 15 dB: +0.029* (stress) and +0.036* (fresh). It does not hold at b = 1 / 0 dB (-0.027* / -0.018*) or at b = 2 (-0.03 to -0.09*).
- **vs the best neural option:** at b <= 2 it is clearly below the fine-tuned CNN read out directly (-0.03 to -0.24*). At b <= 2 the decoder stage, learned or classical, makes things worse. So adapting the decoder does not change the conclusion of finetune/adc: use a bit-aware switch, network-only for b <= 2 and a decoder otherwise. At b >= 3 the best choice is a classical method: consistent NOMP at 15 dB, NOMP / dual / multi-start at 0 dB.
- **Cost on the official subset:** -0.009 [-0.013, -0.005]* vs NOMP and -0.011* vs NOMP os8 rc6. This equals the original learned pipeline, so adapting causes no regression (+0.000 [-0.002, +0.002]). The architecture is unchanged at 4,803 params, so inference cost equals the original learned decoder; latency was not re-measured here.
- **Caveats:**
  - Only the same ADC model was tested: peak AGC, uniform mid-rise quantiser, i.i.d. b ~ U{1..4} in training.
  - True L = 3 is given to every method.
  - The best classical method is picked on the test bank.
  - A 1-bit-aware maximum-likelihood / GAMP baseline is still missing.
  - The training loss barely moved (3.85 to 3.69, noisy), so most of the gain at b <= 2 is a small re-tuning of the refiner, not a large learned change.
